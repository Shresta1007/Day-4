Admission Procedure

Where passion meets purpose

Sumathi Reddy Institute of Technology for Women, established in 2009, is a premier destination for women in technology and engineering. With JNTUH affiliation and NAAC accreditation, the college emphasizes academic excellence, practical learning, and strong career readiness.

Students gain deeper knowledge through well-equipped labs, inspiring faculty, mentorship programs, and campus experiences that help them grow with confidence and skill.
